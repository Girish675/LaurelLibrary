    08 Lakes Of India

    Geography • UPSC, SSC

    North India

    States Notable Lakes Description
    Jammu & Kashmir Dal Lake Known for houseboats, shikaras, and scenic beauty in Srinagar.
    Wular Lake One of the largest freshwater lakes in India, rich in biodiversity.
    Manasbal Lake A deep freshwater lake surrounded by gardens.
    Himachal Pradesh Renuka Lake The largest natural lake in Himachal, shaped like a reclining goddess.
    Rewalsar Lake Sacred to Hindus, Sikhs, and Buddhists, surrounded by temples.
    Chandratal Lake Known as the "Moon Lake" due to its crescent shape, located at high altitudes.
    Gobind Sagar A reservoir formed by the Bhakra Dam, a hub for water sports.
    Uttarakhand Nainital Lake A famous natural freshwater lake, a key attraction of Nainital.
    Bhimtal Lake A larger lake near Nainital, with a small island in the middle.
    Roopkund Lake Known as "Skeleton Lake" due to ancient human remains found near it.
    Punjab Sukhna Lake A man-made lake in Chandigarh, popular for boating and recreation.
    Harike Wetland A Ramsar site at the conful ence of the Beas and Sutlej rivers.
    Haryana Badkhal Lake A man-made lake near Faridabad, now mostly dry but surrounded by hills.
    Karna Lake A scenic lake located near Karnal, popular for picnics.

    South India

    States Notable Lakes Description
    Tamil Nadu Kodaikanal Lake A star-shaped man-made lake in the hill station of Kodaikanal.
    Ooty Lake A man-made lake in Ooty, popular for boating.
    Veeranam Lake A large water reservoir provides water to Chennai.
    Kerala Vembanad Lake The largest lake in Kerala, part of the backwaters, and a key eco-tourism destination.
    Ashtamudi Lake A palm-shaped lake that connects to the Arabian Sea,is known for houseboat cruises.
    Sasthamcotta Lake The largest freshwater lake in Kerala, surrounded by hills.
    States Notable Lakes Description
    Karnataka Ulsoor Lake A large lake in Bengaluru is known for boating and park areas.
    Bellandur Lake The largest lake in Bengaluru, facing pollution issues.
    Agara Lake A rejuvenated lake in Bengaluru, serving as a city waterbody.
    Andhra Pradesh Kolleru Lake One of India's largest freshwater lakes, a haven for migratory birds.
    Pulicat Lake A large saltwater lagoon, famous for fal mingos and other bird species.
    Telangana Hussain Sagar A man-made lake in Hyderabad with a massive Buddha statue in the center.
    Osman Sagar A reservoir created by the damming of the Musi River, supplying drinking water to Hyderabad.
    Durgam Cheruvu Known as the "Secret Lake" near Hyderabad, surrounded by rocky hills.

    Central India

    States Notable Lakes Description
    Madhya Pradesh Bhojtal (Upper Lake) A large man-made lake created by Raja Bhoj in Bhopal, popular for boating.
    Lower Lake Adjacent to Bhojtal, forming the Bhoj Wetland, an important ecological site.
    Tawa Reservoir A large reservoir on the Tawa River ofefrs scenic beauty and boating.
    Chhattisgarh Dalpat Sagar Lake The largest artifciial lake in Chhattisgarh, built for irrigation and fsihing.
    Gangrel Lake A man-made reservoir providing water and tourism opportunities.

    East India

    States Notable Lakes Description
    East Calcutta Wetlands A complex of natural and man-made wetlands, known for wastewater recycling.
    Rabindra Sarobar An artifciial lake in Kolkata, surrounded by parks and sports clubs.

    West Bengal

    Santragachi Lake A shallow lake near Kolkata attracts migratory birds.
    Bihar Kanwar Lake The largest freshwater oxbow lake in Asia, known for its birdlife.
    Odisha Chilika Lake The largest coastal lagoon in India, a Ramsar site famous for migratory birds and dolphins.
    Ansupa Lake A freshwater lake near the Mahanadi River, known for its scenic beauty.
    Assam Deepor Beel A freshwater lake and bird sanctuary near Guwahati, designated as a Ramsar site.
    Hafol ng Lake A beautiful hill station lake in Assam.
    Arunachal Pradesh Sela Lake A high-altitude lake near Sela Pass, often frozen in winter.
    Ganga Lake (Gyakar Sinyi) A scenic lake surrounded by forests, near Itanagar.
    Sikkim Tsomgo Lake A glacial lake near the Indo-China border, sacred to Buddhists.
    Gurudongmar Lake One of the highest lakes in the world, sacred to Sikhs and Buddhists.
    Nagaland Shilloi Lake A natural lake shaped like a footprint is considered sacred.
    Manipur Loktak Lake The largest freshwater lake in Northeast India, known for its fol ating phumdis and Keibul Lamjao National Park.
    Tripura Dumboor Lake A large artifciial lake formed by a dam ofefrs scenic beauty and water sports.
    Meghalaya Umiam Lake A man-made lake created by damming the Umiam River, popular for water sports.
    Jharkhand Dimna Lake An artifciial lake near Jamshedpur is used for drinking water and recreation.
    Chandil Lake A reservoir created by Chandil Dam, surrounded by hills.

    West India

    States Notable Lakes Description
    Rajasthan Sambhar Salt Lake The largest inland salt lake in India, known for its salt production and birdlife.
    Pushkar Lake A sacred lake for Hindus, associated with the town of Pushkar.
    Pichola Lake A scenic lake in Udaipur, famous for its palaces and boat rides.
    Jaisamand Lake The second-largest artifciial lake in India, surrounded by marble cenotaphs.
    Gujarat Nal Sarovar Lake A shallow lake, designated as a bird sanctuary for migratory birds.
    Thol Lake A freshwater lake near Ahmedabad is also a bird sanctuary.
    Goa Carambolim Lake A bird-watching paradise, known for migratory species.
    Mayem Lake A man-made lake popular for boating, located near Bicholim.
    Maharashtra Lonar Lake A unique saltwater lake created by a meteor impact, known for its alkalinity.
    Rankala Lake A natural lake in Kolhapur is popular for its scenic beauty and parks.
    Pashan Lake A man-made lake in Pune, originally created for water supply.

    Jammu & Kashmir (UT) Dal Lake, Wular Lake
