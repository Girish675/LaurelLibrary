About Laurel Library

Laurel Library is a free, open-source collection of study notes for competitive exam aspirants in India.

Supported Exams

  • UPSC - Civil Services, CDS, NDA
  • SSC - CGL, CHSL, MTS
  • GATE - Engineering & Science
  • IIT-JEE - Main & Advanced
  • NEET - Medical Entrance
  • Banking - IBPS, SBI PO/Clerk
  • Railways - RRB NTPC, Group D

Features

  • Clean, distraction-free reading experience
  • Well-organized notes by subject and topic
  • Mobile-friendly design
  • Full-text search across all notes
  • No ads, no paywalls — completely free

How It Works

Notes are curated, cleaned, and formatted into web pages for easy reading. The content is organized by subject and tagged with relevant exams to help you find exactly what you need.

Contributing

This is an open project. If you'd like to contribute notes or suggest improvements, visit our GitHub repository.
