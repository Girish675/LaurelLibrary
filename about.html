About Laurel Library

Laurel Library is a free, open-source competitive exam preparation platform created for Indian students and aspirants. Our mission is simple: make quality study material accessible to everyone — regardless of their financial background.

Our Mission

Competitive exams in India are a gateway to opportunities. But quality preparation material often comes at a steep cost. We believe that shouldn't be the case. Laurel Library provides:

  • Structured study notes covering subjects from Geography to Computer Science
  • Smart study tools — Pomodoro timer, flashcards, GPA calculator, study planner
  • Exam-focused organization — notes tagged by UPSC, SSC, GATE, Banking, Railways
  • Study gamification — XP, streaks, and progress tracking to keep you motivated
  • Offline access — works as a PWA, study anywhere without internet

Technology

Laurel Library is built as a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. No servers, no databases, no backend. All user data (bookmarks, streaks, study time) is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. This means:

  • Blazing fast load times
  • 100% uptime — runs directly from GitHub
  • Zero privacy concerns — we literally can't access your data
  • Completely transparent — read every line of code
